# Quy tac phan tich khuong nhac

File nay la bo quy tac mac dinh de doc va phan tich khuong nhac. Khi nguoi dung gui hinh anh hoac ban nhac, phai uu tien dung file nay de xac dinh tung not, cao do, truong do va khoang nghi mot cach chinh xac.

## Nguyen tac chung

- Khong doan neu hinh anh mo, che khuat, nghieng qua muc, hoac ky hieu khong du ro.
- Neu khong chac chan, phai ghi ro muc do tin cay va noi: "Toi khong the xac dinh chinh xac ky hieu nay do chat luong hinh anh."
- Doc tu trai sang phai, theo tung khuong, tung o nhip.
- Ghi nhan hoa bieu truoc, sau do moi ap dung dau hoa bat thuong trong tung o nhip.
- Dau hoa bat thuong chi co hieu luc voi cung cao do trong cung o nhip, tru khi co natural hoac ket thuc o nhip.
- Phan biet tie va slur:
  - Tie noi hai not cung cao do va cong truong do.
  - Slur chi dan choi lien tieng, khong cong truong do.
- Phan biet beam voi tie/slur. Beam chi noi cac not moc de gom nhip.

## Trinh tu phan tich bat buoc

1. Xac dinh khoa nhac: Sol, Fa, Do alto, Do tenor, hoac khoa khac neu co.
2. Xac dinh hoa bieu: so dau thang hoac dau giang, tu do suy ra giong truong va giong thu kha di.
3. Xac dinh so chi nhip: vi du 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 6/8, 9/8, 12/8.
4. Xac dinh tempo neu co: chu tempo hoac BPM, vi du quarter note = 120.
5. Chia ban nhac theo tung o nhip bang vach nhip.
6. Trong moi o nhip, doc tung not va dau lang theo thu tu.
7. Voi moi not, xac dinh:
   - Vi tri tren khuong hoac dong phu.
   - Cao do ly thuyet theo khoa nhac.
   - Anh huong cua hoa bieu.
   - Anh huong cua dau hoa bat thuong.
   - Ten not theo C D E F G A B va neu can theo Do Re Mi.
   - Truong do theo hinh not, dau cham doi, tie, tuplet.
8. Voi moi dau lang, xac dinh loai dau lang va gia tri thoi gian.
9. Xac dinh ky hieu bieu cam, cuong do, ky thuat bieu dien, lap lai va dieu huong.
10. Kiem tra tong truong do trong moi o nhip co khop voi so chi nhip khong. Neu khong khop, ghi ro nghi van.

## Cao do theo khoa nhac

### Khoa Sol

- Dong tu duoi len: E, G, B, D, F.
- Khe tu duoi len: F, A, C, E.
- Dong thu hai la G.

### Khoa Fa

- Dong tu duoi len: G, B, D, F, A.
- Khe tu duoi len: A, C, E, G.
- Dong thu tu la F.

### Khoa Do

- Khoa Do chi vi tri cua C trung tam.
- Alto clef: C trung tam nam tren dong thu ba.
- Tenor clef: C trung tam nam tren dong thu tu.

## Truong do not

- Whole note / not tron: 4 phach trong nhip co quarter note lam don vi phach.
- Half note / not trang: 2 phach.
- Quarter note / not den: 1 phach.
- Eighth note / not moc don: 1/2 phach.
- Sixteenth note / not moc kep: 1/4 phach.
- Thirty-second note / not moc ba: 1/8 phach.
- Sixty-fourth note / not moc bon: 1/16 phach.

## Dau lang

- Whole rest: nghi bang not tron.
- Half rest: nghi bang not trang.
- Quarter rest: nghi bang not den.
- Eighth rest: nghi bang not moc don.
- Sixteenth rest: nghi bang not moc kep.
- Thirty-second rest: nghi bang not moc ba.

## Cham doi

- Mot cham doi tang them 1/2 gia tri goc.
- Hai cham doi tang them 1/2 + 1/4 gia tri goc.
- Vi du: not trang cham doi = 2 + 1 = 3 phach.

## Dau hoa

- Sharp: tang nua cung.
- Flat: giam nua cung.
- Natural: huy dau hoa va dua not ve trang thai tu nhien trong o nhip.
- Double sharp: tang mot cung.
- Double flat: giam mot cung.

Thu tu uu tien:

1. Hoa bieu.
2. Dau hoa bat thuong trong o nhip.
3. Natural.
4. Ket thuc o nhip thi dau hoa bat thuong het hieu luc.

## Hoa bieu

- Nhan dien tu 0 den 7 dau thang.
- Nhan dien tu 0 den 7 dau giang.
- Neu co the, ghi ca giong truong va giong thu tuong ung.
- Neu khong du thong tin de khang dinh giong, ghi "co kha nang" thay vi khang dinh tuyet doi.

## So chi nhip

- Tu so: so phach trong mot o nhip.
- Mau so: loai not duoc tinh la mot phach.
- Vi du:
  - 2/4: 2 phach, quarter note la mot phach.
  - 3/4: 3 phach, quarter note la mot phach.
  - 4/4: 4 phach, quarter note la mot phach.
  - 6/8: 6 eighth notes moi o nhip, thuong gom 2 cum lon.
  - 9/8: 9 eighth notes moi o nhip, thuong gom 3 cum lon.
  - 12/8: 12 eighth notes moi o nhip, thuong gom 4 cum lon.

## Vach nhip

- Single barline: ket thuc o nhip.
- Double barline: ket thuc doan hoac chuyen phan.
- Final barline: ket thuc ban nhac.
- Repeat start: bat dau doan lap.
- Repeat end: ket thuc doan lap.
- Repeat both: vua ket thuc lap doan truoc vua bat dau lap doan sau.

## Tempo

- Largo: rat cham.
- Adagio: cham.
- Andante: vua phai, nhu buoc di.
- Moderato: vua.
- Allegro: nhanh.
- Vivace: nhanh va linh hoat.
- Presto: rat nhanh.
- Prestissimo: cuc nhanh.
- Neu co BPM, ghi dung ky hieu va con so, vi du quarter note = 120.

## Cuong do

- pp: rat nhe.
- p: nhe.
- mp: hoi nhe.
- mf: hoi manh.
- f: manh.
- ff: rat manh.
- fff: cuc manh.

## Ky hieu bieu cam va ky thuat

- Crescendo: manh dan.
- Diminuendo / decrescendo: nhe dan.
- Accent: nhan manh not.
- Marcato: nhan manh ro, sac.
- Tenuto: giu du gia tri, hoi nhan.
- Staccato: ngan, tach tieng.
- Fermata: ngan/giu lau hon gia tri thong thuong.
- Trill: rung nhanh giua not chinh va not ke ben.
- Mordent: luot nhanh voi not ke ben.
- Turn: nhom not trang tri xoay quanh not chinh.
- Grace note: not trang tri nho, lay thoi gian tu not chinh hoac truoc do.
- Appoggiatura: not dua, thuong co trong luong bieu cam.
- Arpeggio: rai hop am.
- Glissando: luot lien tuc giua hai cao do.

## Tuplet

- Triplet: 3 not trong thoi gian cua 2 not cung loai.
- Duplet: 2 not trong thoi gian cua 3 not cung loai, tuy ngu canh.
- Quintuplet: 5 not trong thoi gian duoc chi dinh.
- Sextuplet: 6 not trong thoi gian duoc chi dinh.
- Luon doc so tuplet va dau ngoac/beam di kem neu co.

## Octave marks

- 8va: choi cao hon mot quang tam.
- 8vb: choi thap hon mot quang tam.
- 15ma: choi cao hon hai quang tam.
- 15mb: choi thap hon hai quang tam.

## Repeat va dieu huong

- D.C. / Da Capo: quay lai dau ban nhac.
- D.S. / Dal Segno: quay lai dau Segno.
- Fine: ket thuc tai day.
- Coda: doan ket rieng.
- Segno: dau moc de quay lai.
- Dal Segno al Fine: quay lai Segno va choi den Fine.
- Dal Segno al Coda: quay lai Segno, den dau Coda thi nhay sang Coda.
- Da Capo al Fine: quay lai dau va choi den Fine.
- Da Capo al Coda: quay lai dau, den dau Coda thi nhay sang Coda.

## Quy tac khi hinh anh khong ro

- Neu khong thay du khoa nhac, khong duoc suy cao do tuyet doi.
- Neu khong thay du hoa bieu, phai ghi "hoa bieu khong xac dinh ro".
- Neu than not, duoi not, beam, hoac dau cham bi mo, phai ghi "truong do khong chac chan".
- Neu not nam tren dong phu nhung dong phu khong ro, phai ghi "cao do khong chac chan".
- Neu co nhieu cach doc hop ly, liet ke cac kha nang va muc do tin cay.
